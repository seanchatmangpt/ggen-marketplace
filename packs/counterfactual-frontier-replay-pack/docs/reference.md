# Reference: counterfactual frontier replay

A candidate is admissible to the replayable frontier only when it is qualified, current, non-actuating, receipt-bound, and has standing `ALIVE` or `PARTIAL_ALIVE`. The optimization dimensions are reversibility, expected reuse, and expected capability-space delta. Dominance requires weak improvement on all three dimensions and strict improvement on at least one. Compositions preserve membership, order, branching factor, and replay key. This pack has no DO authority.
