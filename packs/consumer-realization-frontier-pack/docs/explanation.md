# Explanation: option capital at the consumer boundary

Producer qualification does not imply independent consumer realization. This pack models that gap as a composable frontier rather than a binary rollout task. Each axis is independently extensible, so one new verifier, relief mechanism, runtime surface, receipt form, or consumer creates new lawful compositions without discarding existing non-dominated alternatives.

The design separates structural leverage from observed leverage. The ontology can prove that thousands of candidate combinations are reachable from admitted facts; only execution against exact consumer subjects can prove consumer standing. This keeps combinatorial maximalism compatible with evidence discipline and preserves BRCE as the exclusive consequential boundary.
