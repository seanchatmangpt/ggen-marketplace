# Revenue structural-option factory

The factory preserves a non-dominated executable frontier rather than selecting a single application architecture. Its canonical path is `ExistingStructure -> CandidateStructures[] -> PackOptions[] -> Experiments/Falsifiers -> GeneratedConsequences -> Consumers -> Qualifications -> AgentRefinement -> UpstreamLearning -> Regeneration -> Supersession`.

`RevenueFROMCustomer` models the producer's payer, offer, distribution, stream, retention, and expansion topology. `RevenueFORCustomer` models capability-to-customer-process-to-value-outcome-to-customer-revenue-outcome topology. They are intentionally distinct and composable.

Structural cardinality is option capital, not observed revenue. A candidate receives standing only from its applicable falsifiers and exact-subject qualification. SELECT projections have no ambient DO authority.
