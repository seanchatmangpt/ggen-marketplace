# Manufacturing scale search: 100 / 250 / 500+

CELL3 does not stop because the 50-commit floor was crossed. It searches larger lawful envelopes and refuses artificial splitting.

## 100-commit envelope

A 100+ semantic-commit allocation envelope is structurally available only after current exact evidence exists for more targets. Candidate capital includes per-target current-subject rebind contracts, target-specific qualification plans, typed repair/re-entry manifests, exact OCEL ManufacturingObject/Event bindings, and reusable consumer scaffolds. Those become lawful commits only when they bind observed current subjects and real owning courts. Creating them now for evidence-missing targets would convert UNKNOWN into invented structure.

## 250-commit envelope

A 250+ envelope is reachable by composing multiple independently admitted target families with the legal selector pack: currentness gates, authority adapters, qualification courts, replay guards, process-observation bindings, and shared upstream-return factories. The multiplier is target-specific exact evidence. Until the mandatory Project2 reads and canonical current-run OCEL observation complete, expanding this envelope would increase MURI and speculation rather than qualified fanout.

## 500+ envelope

A 500+ envelope requires demonstrated recurring variation sufficient to justify a factory-for-factories or new generator family across many consumers. Current evidence supports one reusable legal-allocation pack and several repair/rebind edges, but not 500 distinct non-vacuous semantic mutations. Manufacturing hundreds of near-identical target files merely to satisfy volume would violate semantic independence and generated-source ownership.

## Current limiting multipliers

1. canonical current-run OCEL generation is fail-closed on semantic projection-reference continuity;
2. several mandatory Project2 pre-score reads do not yet have independent receipts;
3. current ForcedTop25 `consumer_ready_set` is empty;
4. target-specific exact-current rebind evidence is missing for multiple historically qualified consumers;
5. `cargo-cicd` remains authority-fenced.

The preserved next expansion frontier is therefore explicit rather than discarded: repair the shared OCEL semantic edge, finish mandatory reads, re-evaluate exact current admission, then instantiate target-specific contracts only for newly admitted candidates. This stop condition is evidence-bounded, not quota-bounded.
