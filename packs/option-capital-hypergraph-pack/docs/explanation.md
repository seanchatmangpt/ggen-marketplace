# Why option capital is a hypergraph

A task list loses the fact that new capability can arise from compositions rather than isolated nodes. This pack therefore represents each executable alternative as a candidate over a reversible composition, and binds that candidate to a canonical opportunity edge.

Pairwise edges expose immediate adjacency; third-order compositions expose capability that only exists when several factories cooperate. The Pareto projection deliberately does not choose one winner. It preserves every source candidate that remains executable and non-dominated, while the ledger retains the broader admitted topology for replay and later re-ranking.

Qualification is not ambient authority. `SELECT`, `CONSTRUCT`, and `VERIFY` may manufacture evidence and projections; consequential DO remains outside this pack and must pass its own BRCE boundary.
