# Why selection calibration is manufacturing capital

A lawful selector can still allocate manufacturing effort poorly. R25 separates selection from realization; R26 closes the next loop by measuring how often selected edges realize value, how often observed beneficial alternatives were missed, whether dependency relief justified investment, and whether apparently independent evidence actually shares one root.

This is capital rather than reporting because the outputs alter future manufacturing allocation. Precision protects against false-capital investment. Observed-alternative recall protects against systematic missed opportunity. Root-discounted yield prevents correlated evidence from inflating confidence. Policy-churn detection prevents repeated policy manufacture when realized value does not improve.

The layer is intentionally non-actuating. It observes, verifies, and supplies SELECT evidence. BRCE remains the exclusive consequential DO path.
