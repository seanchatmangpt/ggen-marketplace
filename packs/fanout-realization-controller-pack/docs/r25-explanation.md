# Why control realization is a separate epistemic-capital layer

A controller can lawfully SELECT an edge from current evidence and still be empirically poor. Selection correctness is therefore not inherited from controller correctness.

R25 separates five objects: the decision, the selected realized outcome, independently observed alternatives, the policy generation, and the evidence-root/receipt topology. This prevents three common forms of epistemic inflation: treating predictions as outcomes, inventing counterfactual regret from unexecuted alternatives, and counting correlated evidence roots as independent confirmation.

The measurement layer remains upstream of consequence. Its role is to improve future observation and selection by exposing false-positive selection, prediction error, realized dependency relief, cost/latency economics, policy churn, stale/split-current evidence, and negative worst strata. It has no ambient DO authority.
