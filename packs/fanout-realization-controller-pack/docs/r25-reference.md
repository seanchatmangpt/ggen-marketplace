# R25 control-realization reference

## Identity
`ControlDecision` requires exact subject, selected edge, predicted qualified actions, predicted dependency relief, predicted cost/latency, policy digest, generation, currentness, receipt, SELECT authority, and no actuation.

`ControlOutcome` requires its originating decision, edge, selected/observed-alternative status, realized qualified actions, dependency relief, cost/latency, evidence root, standing, exact subject, currentness, receipt, VERIFY authority, and no actuation.

## Measurement families
The 40 R25 queries cover census/coverage, prediction error, efficiency, false selection, observed alternatives/regret, relief, evidence-root capital, freshness/frontiers, exact identity, receipt/authority/actuation integrity, worst strata, discovery multiplier E, net value, policy churn, provenance, generation yield, support, and a clean current court.

## Standing
R25 measures control quality. It never promotes a consumer, repository, or global Crown by inheritance. Positive generated court output is capped at `PARTIAL_ALIVE` unless an owning higher-level court independently establishes stronger standing.
