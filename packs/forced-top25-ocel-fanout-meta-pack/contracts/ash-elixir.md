# Ash/Elixir consumer composition contract

Consumers using Ash, Reactor, Phoenix or LiveView must receive generated source through the canonical GGEN pack path, never hand-edited generated outputs. Required OCEL events are `admit`, `select`, `generate`, `realize`, and typed `refuse`. Exact consumer head and exact GGEN producer head are mandatory before generation. Product/runtime changes may satisfy downstream realization; Project-memory request/receipt traffic is CONTROL. Qualification preserves policies, migrations, tests, UI projections and `VERIFY|CONSTRUCT` authority without ambient DO.
