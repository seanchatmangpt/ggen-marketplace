# Python/process consumer composition contract

Python consumers may receive generated adapters, schemas, CLI/API seams, validators, deployment descriptors and deterministic evidence projections. Process discovery, conformance, fitness, precision, variants and drift are excluded from this pack and remain in wasm4pm/wasm4pm-compat. Generated artifacts enumerate validators so closure does not depend on hand-maintained CI lists. Exact source/consumer identities and qualification receipts bind standing.
