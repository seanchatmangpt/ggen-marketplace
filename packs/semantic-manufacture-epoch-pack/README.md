# Semantic Manufacture Epoch Pack

## v26.9.14 — constitutional cutover

The semantic graph is canonical. Generated artifacts are projections.
Handwritten repair of generated projections is refused. Planners have no
authority, generators are capped at `CONSTRUCT`, consequence-bearing `DO`
remains external, and receipt/replay evidence is mandatory.

## v26.9.15 — machine-native ephemerals

Human readability and persistence are no longer projection requirements.
A generated implementation may exist only long enough to be verified,
authorized by an external consequence boundary, executed there, receipted,
and retired.

Every declared `sm:Projection` must carry exact `sm:graphDigest`,
`sm:projectionDigest`, and `sm:manufacturerDigest` identities. Those
identities establish provenance and replay scope; they do not grant authority.

This pack remains a semantic/admission pack. It does not create a new
top-level authority pack or a new DO path.
