# Why causality belongs in the manufacturing capital loop

A busy control plane can produce many workflows, commits, and receipts without proving that remembered state caused useful manufacture. Raw fanout therefore cannot be used as either memory multiplier `M` or manufacturing yield `Y`.

R68 fences that error by requiring a current exact request, a relevant workflow that actually used it, and a current ALIVE receipt generated by that workflow. It separately exposes stale memory, unrelated workflow fanout, missing provenance, identity mismatch, and correlated evidence roots.

This composes with R67 rather than replacing it. R67 asks whether consumer evidence is independent across domains; R68 asks whether the control-plane path producing or observing that evidence is causally grounded. Together they prevent two different multiplier inflations: correlated consumers and correlated workflow noise.

The pack remains observational. A clean causal chain may inform SELECT or CONSTRUCT, but it grants no ambient DO authority. Consequential action remains behind BRCE and its own receipt/replay boundary.
