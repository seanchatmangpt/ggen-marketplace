# Qualification — premature-actuation-survival-pack

The pack is declarative and grants no runtime authority. Its gates are
violation-row SELECT queries: zero rows is clean; one or more rows is a typed
refusal candidate for external admission machinery.

## Exact-head court

The pack workflow asserts the exact PR head, validates marketplace structure,
runs the independent SHACL/catalog court, validates gate↔witness
correspondence, executes every gate against both witnesses, and checks that
shipped positive evidence contains no ambient DO grant.

The gate case count is derived from the number of gates rather than hard-coded,
so adding a gate without a corresponding witness cannot silently pass.

## Anti-vacuity gates

Each gate has an exact-stem passing witness and failing witness.

1. wrong-subject DO
2. missing authority or admission on DO
3. unreceipted DO
4. DO before terminal readiness
5. policy claiming ambient DO authority
6. guard candidate carrying authority or claiming installation
7. unknown/missing policy machinery
8. incomplete scenario identity/horizon
9. campaign profile with authority, implicit synthetic identity, or missing receipt/replay obligation
10. incomplete/inconsistent analysis-method contract

The semantic runner executes **all gates** for every witness. A pass witness
must fire none. A fail witness must fire its target gate and no other gate.

## Catalog court

`qualification/verify_catalog.py` is independent of ggen rendering. It parses
the ontology and shapes directly, runs SHACL, runs every refusal gate against
the canonical ontology, then checks exact catalog closure:

- 5 machinery policies
- 4 factors × 3 levels = 12 factor levels
- 8 typed fault kinds
- 6 analysis methods
- powerless contract ownership and receipt/replay bindings

## Generated projections

`ggen.toml` defines four ontology projections: policies, factor levels,
fault kinds, and analysis methods. Generated bytes are replaceable; the RDF
ontology remains the source of truth.

## Boundary

The pack does not execute a GymAct case and does not itself compute
Kaplan-Meier curves, RMST, confidence intervals, log-rank statistics, or CUSUM.

- `autofde-lab` owns survival/process/statistical analysis.
- `gymact` owns scenario × policy × factor manufacture and deterministic
  fault campaigns.
- BRCE remains the only lawful consequential DO path.

A clean marketplace court establishes only the semantics of the reusable pack.
It does not transfer runtime or production standing from any consumer.
