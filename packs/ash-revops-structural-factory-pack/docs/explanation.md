# Why this pack exists

The unit of capital is not a chosen application implementation; it is a reversible structural option that can be deterministically projected into many qualified consequences. The factory therefore keeps Ash, Reactor, UI, OCEL, revenue, qualification, and regeneration dimensions separable until evidence justifies selection.

Downstream consumers also become sensors. Their refinements can produce generalizable knowledge, which becomes new marketplace source, regenerates compatible consumers, and forces requalification. This closes the manufacturing-learning loop without granting observations or templates execution authority.
