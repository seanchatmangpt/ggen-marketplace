# fde20-revops-pack

Ontology-first ggen pack for the **$20/hr FDE RevOps — Fortune 5 profile**.

Enterprise readiness is executable graph closure, not a checklist. The pack manufactures:

- `revops/fde20/OPERATING_CONTRACT.md` — economics, authority calculus, SLOs, deployment envelope, and 12-stage operating contract.
- `revops/fde20/CHALLENGER_MOTION.md` — Fortune 5 Challenger commercial motion.
- `revops/fde20/FORTUNE5_READINESS.md` — 8 authority roles, 12 readiness dimensions, 36 controls, acceptance evidence, receipts, and standing ceilings.
- `revops/fde20/pipeline.json` — machine-readable pipeline, authorities, controls, SLO profile, and deployment invariants.

The readiness dimensions cover executive governance, commercial/procurement, identity/security, data/privacy, platform/cloud, software supply chain, reliability/observability, continuity/recovery, change/release, FinOps/capacity, process intelligence, and portability/exit.

The reference envelope requires at least two regions, multi-cloud capability, tenant isolation, infrastructure as code, rollback, SBOM, signed provenance, observability, and OCEL v2. The service-level floor is availability >= 99.9%, RTO <= 240 minutes, RPO <= 60 minutes, and at least 365 days of audit/receipt retention.

`SELECT` chooses an admitted route. `CONSTRUCT` manufactures reversible artifacts and intents. `DO` changes external state. Every DO stage must pass BRCE and emit a receipt binding identity, authority, consequence, replay, and standing.

The pack does **not** actuate CRM, email, billing, cloud, deployment, or customer systems. Downstream gdmcp/AutoFDE/BRCE adapters remain the only authority-bearing DO path.
