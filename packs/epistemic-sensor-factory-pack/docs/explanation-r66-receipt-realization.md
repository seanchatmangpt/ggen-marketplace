# Explanation: why receipt realization is epistemic capital

A consumer target, a consumer implementation, a successful execution, a returned receipt, and an assimilated portfolio fact are different states. Treating them as one collapses the production graph and systematically overstates replication.

R66 makes those transitions observable. It compares predicted fanout with realized exact consumer subjects, measures how many execution receipts return, how many are assimilated into the evidence graph, whether the roots are actually independent, and whether predicted opportunity yield and dependency relief occur afterward.

This changes the production function because future cells can allocate sensing and replication capital from realized evidence instead of reconstructing consumer state manually. It also turns missing or delayed receipt return into a typed manufacturing opportunity.

R66 remains observational. SELECT may consume its projections; CONSTRUCT may manufacture consumers; only the separately authorized consequential boundary may DO. No R66 graph fact grants ambient execution authority.
