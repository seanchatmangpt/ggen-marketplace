# Tutorial: inspect the independent consumer fanout frontier

R55 treats a merged consumer as an independently grounded subject, not as inherited producer standing. Start with `fixtures/r55-independent-consumer-fanout.ttl`, then execute `tests/run_r55_independent_consumer_fanout.py`. The court loads the canonical ontology plus the four exact consumer observations and runs sensors 651–700.

The expected teaching result is deliberately asymmetric: four consumer subjects are visible, three are admitted at the consumer boundary, but only one currently has the complete producer-return surface required for independent readiness. That difference is the manufacturing frontier.
