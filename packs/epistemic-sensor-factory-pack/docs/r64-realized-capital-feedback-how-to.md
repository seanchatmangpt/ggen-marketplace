# How to qualify realized manufacturing capital

1. Admit the exact post-allocation `ProductionFunctionObservation` subject.
2. Require a positive frontier delta and non-zero downstream opportunity yield.
3. Check capability reuse separately from opportunity count; duplicated output is not reusable capital.
4. Require independent evidence roots and sufficient factor evidence before admitting multiplier claims.
5. Require receipts, replay verification, and explicit standing on the realized observation.
6. Inspect observation, reuse, and opportunity factors individually before admitting their product.
7. Run `python3 packs/epistemic-sensor-factory-pack/tests/run_r64_realized_capital_feedback.py` and marketplace validation.
