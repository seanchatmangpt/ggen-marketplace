# Explanation: why propagation changes the production function

R50 closes the loop from a consumer back to the producer. R51 composes that receipt into the next observation frontier. The architectural shift is from centrally rediscovering every consumer to compiling exact downstream targets from already qualified consumer evidence. That makes breadth multiplicative only when independently receipted; it does not manufacture ALIVE by transitivity.

The system therefore preserves the distinction between evidence propagation and authority propagation: evidence may fan out, authority does not. SELECT/CONSTRUCT may manufacture plans; consequential DO remains outside this pack and behind BRCE.
