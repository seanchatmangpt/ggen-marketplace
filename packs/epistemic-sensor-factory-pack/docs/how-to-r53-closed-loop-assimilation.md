1. Resolve producer and consumer default refs to exact SHAs.
2. Require independent exact-head execution evidence from the consumer.
3. Bind consumer base/head, producer court head, merge SHA, containing default head, evidence roots, replay result, and consequential authority in RDF.
4. Run `test_r53_closed_loop_assimilation.py` and `run_r53_sparql.py`.
5. Render the ggen assimilation and next-wave projections from the ontology/query/template editing surfaces.
6. Promote standing only when receipt, checks, replay, containment, and no-ambient-DO evidence agree.
7. Feed only non-ALIVE exact subjects into the next wave. Keep BUILD_BROKEN, PARTIAL_ALIVE, UNKNOWN, and typed REFUSED distinct.
