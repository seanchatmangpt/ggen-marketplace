# Explanation: why fanout is not independence

Repository fanout can grow while epistemic coverage remains almost unchanged. Consumers that share runtime, failure mode, authority boundary, receipt mechanism, or qualification rail may fail together; counting them as independent evidence overstates confidence and corrupts 1000X accounting.

R67 makes common-cause structure observable. A consumer is valuable not only because it executes a producer contract, but because it exercises mechanisms capable of falsifying different assumptions. Capital can therefore be allocated toward marginal evidence-root diversity instead of raw adoption count.

Correlated consumers remain lawful options. Correlation changes epistemic weight and selection priority, not option existence. Consequential DO remains outside this measurement surface.
