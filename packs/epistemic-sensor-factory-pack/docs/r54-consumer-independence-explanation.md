# Explanation: why fanout is not independence

Repository fanout can grow while epistemic coverage remains almost unchanged. Two consumers that share the same runtime, failure mode, authority boundary, receipt mechanism, and qualification rail may fail together; multiplying them as independent evidence overstates confidence and corrupts 1000X accounting.

R54 turns this hidden correlation into observable graph structure. A consumer is valuable not only because it executes the producer contract, but because it exercises different mechanisms capable of falsifying different assumptions. The result changes the production function: replication can now be allocated toward marginal evidence-root diversity instead of raw adoption count.

This does not eliminate correlated consumers. DfCM preserves lawful non-dominated breadth. Correlation changes epistemic weight and selection priority, not the existence of the option. Consequential DO remains outside the measurement surface and restricted to the ecosystem's admitted actuation boundary.
