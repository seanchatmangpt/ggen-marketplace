# Explanation — Why receipt assimilation changes the production function

A replication plan, generated consumer surface, successful consumer court, and relieved ecosystem dependency are different epistemic objects. R61 preserves those boundaries and measures conversion between them. This converts fanout from a count of manufactured artifacts into causal capital accounting: which independently grounded consumer execution actually changed the reachable capability frontier, at what latency, with what replay/currentness, and with how many downstream opportunities.
