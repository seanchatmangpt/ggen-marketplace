# Explanation: Why adapter capital is a production-function multiplier

R56 proved a reusable replication-qualification surface but left consumer breadth at 2/10. R57 moves one layer downstream: instead of reasoning separately about each consumer, it canonicalizes the dimensions that determine whether a target-local adapter can be manufactured and qualified.

This preserves heterogeneity. Rust CLI, Elixir control-plane, Elixir process-intelligence, and simulation-gym consumers keep their irreducible runtime boundaries while sharing one semantic admission and selection calculus. The result is a common producer-owned court with small consumer-local adapters, rather than duplicated verifier implementations.

The production-function gain is prospective until independently qualified consumer receipts return. R57 therefore reports gap topology and deterministic next candidates but does not propagate standing, authority, or 1000X claims across repository boundaries.
