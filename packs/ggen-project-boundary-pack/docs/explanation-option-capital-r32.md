# Explanation: why EXPLORE preserves a frontier

EXPLORE manufactures option capital: qualified reversible ways the ecosystem could evolve. Collapsing to one winner early destroys information about complementarity, dependency relief, independent evidence, and cross-repository reuse. R32 therefore separates candidate realization from calibration and allocation. Realization says an option exists; calibration asks whether its score survives an independent oracle; allocation uses relief, complementarity, and redundancy only after those facts have standing.

This structure makes verification generative. A failed edge becomes a typed topology update or a new missing primitive, while unaffected alternatives remain available. No candidate gains DO authority merely by ranking highly.
