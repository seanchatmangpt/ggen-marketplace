# Generated option-yield calibration

R19B composes the installed manufacturing-capital ledger with deterministic ggen projection. It deliberately does not choose a winner: it manufactures a comparable option-yield surface from net capital growth, capital productivity, compositional commit share, and CAPEX share. The generated court qualifies arithmetic invariants only; policy, authority, and consequential actuation remain outside this projection.

The option-yield expression is an exploration heuristic, not an empirical truth claim. Its falsifiers reject invalid denominators, nonpositive closing stock, and impossible commit shares. Alternative calibration formulae remain lawful adjacent candidates.
