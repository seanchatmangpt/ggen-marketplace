# Tutorial: allocate the next independent consumer

Start from admitted consumer candidates carrying exact-subject, qualification, replay, falsifier, independence, cost, yield and unlock evidence. The R71 pack executes 51 SPARQL courts, then deterministically projects only clean independent candidates into a consumer manufacturing plan. Correlated and stale candidates remain visible as falsifiers rather than being silently deleted. Consequential DO is excluded; the generated plan is an intent for downstream qualification, not authority to actuate.
