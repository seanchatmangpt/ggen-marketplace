# Why revalidation is manufacturing capital

Temporal truth alone detects stale knowledge. R73 closes the next morphism: stale/current evidence becomes a deterministic plan for what must be requalified before capital can flow.

This matters because an ALIVE claim bound to an obsolete head is not merely inaccurate metadata; it suppresses lawful manufacture by either authorizing the wrong subject or forcing rediscovery. R73 preserves the option while requiring exact-subject revalidation.

The pack therefore treats revalidation as capital allocation: rank by downstream yield and unlocks, discount authority and correlation cost, preserve reversible alternatives, and refuse ambient DO. A generated plan is still CONSTRUCT, never DO.
