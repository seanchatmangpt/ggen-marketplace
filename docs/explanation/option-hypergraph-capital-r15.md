# Why option hypergraphs increase manufacturing capital

A task list collapses possibilities into queued selections. A capability hypergraph instead preserves lawful compositions among reusable capabilities, public ontologies, marketplace packs, repositories, and qualification surfaces. R15 makes those compositions executable as queryable candidates while retaining dominated or failed adjacent edges as topology evidence. The economic object is therefore not a single chosen feature but an expanding stock of reversible, qualified manufacturing possibilities. The exploration multiplier measures candidate creation per consumed actionable opportunity; capability-space delta and expected reuse capture additional future manufacturing capacity without granting ambient execution authority.
