# Crown Gap Hardening

The marketplace is the semantic supply plane. Crown-gap hardening closes the distance between **a capability being named or generated** and **the exact boundary actually earning standing**.

Hard laws:

- semantic identity is canonical and provenance-bound;
- operator applicability/preconditions are explicit data rather than hidden allowlists;
- generated projections do not grant DO authority;
- incomplete executable coverage remains `UNKNOWN`, `PARTIAL_ALIVE`, `BLOCKED`, `UNSUPPORTED`, or typed `REFUSED` as appropriate rather than being rounded up to ALIVE;
- downstream projections derive from admitted graph/configuration facts rather than hand-maintained parallel registries;
- exact-subject execution is required for the boundary being crowned;
- documentation correspondence is part of Level-5 closure, not a substitute for execution;
- class closure removes duplicated semantic authority without erasing non-equivalent runtimes/worlds/compatibility seams.

The Level-5 crown is therefore not one green job. It is the dependency-closed composition of the maturity dimensions actually claimed:

```text
semantic source
× admission
× manufacture
× execution
× receipt/replay
× authority fence
× composition
× Diátaxis correspondence
```
