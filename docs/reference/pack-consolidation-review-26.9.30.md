# Reference: pack consolidation review 26.9.30

A read-only, evidence-based review of every pack in the marketplace for consolidation, upgrade, and replacement leads. It is a **dated receipt for a fixed subject**, not a live source: the authoritative flags are in [`lifecycle.toml`](pack-lifecycle-registry.md) and the catalog.

## Subject and method

- Subject: `4e73cf49a091845ee7eacaf1a0fae4433b49e6ab` (381 packs).
- Nothing was deleted, moved, merged, or modified; the only repository changes are `lifecycle.toml` and this page.
- Pass 1: each pack family was compared from source (RDF classes/properties by IRI and local name, template `to:` targets, byte-identical files, gates, `ggen.toml`, `qualification/baseline.json`, consumers). Names and descriptions were not treated as evidence: the keyword census in `docs/reference/legacy-pack-census-26.9.12.json` had already been refuted 44/45 times on sampled rows and was not used.
- Pass 2: every flag that would be recorded was re-examined by three independent skeptics with different lenses (evidence, consumer/authority, minimality). A flag survives only if a majority confirm it; otherwise it is recorded at the weakest intent the skeptics support.
- Pass 3: pack pairs in different review groups with a structural lead (shared local vocabulary, colliding `to:` target, byte-identical file) were each reviewed on their own.
