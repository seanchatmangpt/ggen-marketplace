# Reference: standing

Marketplace evidence uses bounded claims.

`ALIVE` may be asserted for a specific repository head when the exact-head CI has executed and passed the admitted marketplace acceptance contract.

That standing covers structural pack admission, deterministic catalog projection, path-safety checks, the required Diátaxis surface, and whatever other gates the exact workflow actually executed.

It does **not** imply that every pack has been executed against every consumer, that generated systems are correct, that external APIs/clouds were actuated, that benchmark SOTA was achieved, or that BRCE DO authority exists.

A queued workflow, draft PR, authored validator, or historical success at another SHA does not establish current `ALIVE` standing.
