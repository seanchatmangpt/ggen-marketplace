# R73 Revalidation Manufacturing Capital Reference

`revalidation-manufacturing-capital-pack` composes R72 temporal-truth evidence into an authority-bounded manufacturing plan.

Inputs are exact-subject candidate facts: observed/live heads, standing, revalidation requirement, reversibility, authority cost, dependency/composition unlocks, consumer fanout, expected yield, information gain, future capacity, and evidence requirements.

The pack owns 51 executable SPARQL courts. Court 051 is the deterministic ggen projection. Consequential actuation is prohibited; the generated JSON is a plan, not authority to execute.

Admission requires receipt, replay, falsifier, deterministic manufacture, exact qualification, reversibility, and `consequentialDo=false`. `1000X` remains refused unless independent reusable, consumer, and composition factors are all observed at or above 10.
